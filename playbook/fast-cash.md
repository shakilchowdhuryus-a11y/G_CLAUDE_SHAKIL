# Fast-cash plan: sell the Venue Audit fix

Nothing here guarantees income. It's the shortest path from assets you have
(niche, audit tool, Reels skill) to paid work: a low-ticket offer to warm venues,
upsold into a retainer.

## Offer ladder
| Step | Offer | Price (suggested) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Free audit score (the tool) | £0 — lead capture |
| 2 | Done-for-you fix: top 5 fixes in 14 days | £497 one-off |
| 3 | 4 Reels/month + enquiry follow-up system | £997/month |

Guarantee for step 2: "If enquiries don't rise in 60 days, we keep working free until they do."

## 7-day sprint
- **Day 1:** Put your booking link in `projects/venue-audit/index.html`, host it (GitHub Pages). List 50 venues (Google Maps "wedding venue" + your county; Instagram).
- **Day 2–4:** Send 15 DMs/emails a day (scripts below). Run 3 venues through the audit yourself and send them their score.
- **Day 3+:** Post 1 Reel a day: "3 reasons your venue's enquiries are dropping" → end card "Comment AUDIT".
- **Day 5–7:** Follow up everyone who hasn't replied. Book calls; pitch step 2 on the call.
Target: 50 contacts → ~10 replies → ~4 calls → 1–2 sales.

## Scripts
**Cold DM / email**
> Hi {name}, I ran {venue} through our 16-point wedding venue marketing audit — you scored {score}/100. The biggest leak: {top fix}. Want the full list? Takes 2 minutes to go through.

**Follow-up (day 3)**
> Quick nudge, {name} — the fix for "{top fix}" alone usually adds enquiries within a month. Happy to send the other 4.

**Call close**
> Those 5 fixes are worth doing. You can DIY them from the list, or we implement all five in 14 days for £497, guaranteed. Want me to start Monday?
